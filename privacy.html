Privacy

Themis Library stores study progress and saved activity locally in the browser unless an account and server sync are used. Account information is used to provide authentication and synced profile features. AI requests may be sent to the configured AI provider when a production AI provider is enabled.

Data minimization

Do not enter confidential, privileged, or personally sensitive legal information into Themis Library AI. Themis Library is an educational study platform, not a legal-services provider.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Themis Library project owner through the public contact channel listed on the deployed site.
